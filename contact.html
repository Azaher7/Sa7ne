Before You Order
💳
Payment — 50% Upfront
All orders require a 50% deposit upfront to begin production. The remaining 50% is due prior to dispatch. This applies to all custom and ready-to-order pieces.
🇦🇪
UAE Delivery Only
We currently deliver within the United Arab Emirates only. Delivery is arranged after production is complete and the balance is settled.
🏺
Pre-Order Production
All Sa7ne pieces are made to order. Delivery follows 2–4 weeks from the date of confirmed order and deposit received.
