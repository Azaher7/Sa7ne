Our story

Made by hand,
in small & loving batches.

Sa7ne — صحني — means "my plate" in Arabic: the dish you reach for first, the one that carries the morning's fruit and the evening's bread to the centre of the table.

Every piece begins as a ball of raw stoneware and is shaped entirely by hand — pinched, coiled and smoothed until it holds the gentle imperfections that make it one of a kind. We work in small batches, glaze in soft butter-yellows and sky-blues, and fire each piece slowly so it lasts for years of gathering.

The making

From raw clay to your table.

No moulds, no shortcuts — just clay, hands, fire and time. Here is how every Sa7ne piece comes to life.

  1. 01

    Design

    Every form starts with a sketch and a question: how will it be held, filled and shared? Proportion comes first.

  2. 02

    Handcrafting

    Each piece is shaped entirely by hand — thrown on the wheel or built coil by coil, then smoothed and refined.

  3. 03

    Firing

    Pieces are bisque-fired, hand-glazed in our soft palette, then fired again slowly to over 1,200°C for lasting strength.

  4. 04

    Finishing

    Foot rings are sanded smooth, each piece is checked by hand and signed before it sets off to find its home.
