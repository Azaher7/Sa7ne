Our Story
Your Table Tells Your
Guests Your Story

Sa7ne — صَحْني, meaning "my plate" in Arabic — is a ceramics studio dedicated to the storytelling of a homeowner through tablescapes. Every piece is handmade, customised, and built around colour, form, and meaning — so that the table you set speaks before a single word is said.

Through a collection of personalised dinnerware, Sa7ne personalises the act of hosting. It captures the personality and vocabulary of the dinner host and hostess — their colour sensibility, their warmth, their humour, their heritage — and translates it into ceramic form. A table set with Sa7ne pieces is a table that could only belong to you.

Sa7ne also helps homeowners visualise their tablescape through moodboards — composing Sa7ne pieces into a curated visual so you can see your home's story told before a single piece is made.
