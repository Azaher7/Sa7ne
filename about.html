Our Story
Your Table Tells Your Guests Your Story
Sa7ne is not just dinnerware. It is the vocabulary of your home — handcrafted, personalised, and entirely yours.
The Origin
A Gift I Didn't Expect

I am a full-time lawyer. My days were spent inside the corporate 9-to-6, where stress was folded into my everyday routine. Ceramics quickly became my therapeutic outlet — a scheduled weekend ritual I found myself looking forward to more than almost anything else.

As my experience with clay grew, my pieces became more distinct — more unique, more me. My friends started noticing, and started asking me to dress their tables with my pieces. What had started as a personal outlet was slowly becoming something more.

One night, after an afternoon spent at the ceramics studio, I stopped to pray Isha at a beautiful mosque nearby — a striking building tucked, almost improbably, into the heart of industrial Al Quoz, its exterior engraved with Quranic verses. For months, across many visits, I never looked closely enough to read them properly. I assumed the calligraphy running along the walls was Allah's 99 names — not an unreasonable guess, given that the interior of the Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi is adorned the same way. The word I recalled reading each time was الرحمان — Ar-Rahman.

That night, out of curiosity, I finally looked up what was actually written. It wasn't the 99 names — it was Surah Ar-Rahman, which happens to be my favourite surah. Reading through its verses line by line, I came across one I had somehow never paid attention to before, verse 14:

خَلَقَ الْإِنسَانَ مِنْ صَلْصَالٍ كَالْفَخَّارِ

"He created man from sounding clay, like pottery." — Surah Ar-Rahman, 55:14

How curious, that this verse should sit inside my favourite surah — and that I should find it on the very night I needed to. Allah places our inclinations, our temperaments, and His words exactly where they need to be. سبحان الله. Making that connection felt like a gift I hadn't asked for — هذا من فضل ربي, a favour of my Lord. That night I decided to launch Sa7ne.

The Philosophy
Hosting Is Storytelling

There is a particular kind of host whose table you remember long after the evening ends. It is not the food alone — it is the feeling of intention. The sense that every object was chosen, that the colors speak to each other, that the table itself has a voice. Sa7ne exists to give that voice a form.

We think of each collection as a chapter in the homeowner's story. The blush speckled bowl that always holds the season's fruit. The honey-glazed platter that appears at every gathering. The cake stand painted with stars and the Arabic word for sustenance — رزق — set out on the night of a celebration. These pieces are not interchangeable. They belong to a specific home, a specific host, a specific way of being in the world.

Sa7ne captures the personality and vocabulary of the dinner host and hostess — their colour sensibility, their warmth, their humour, their heritage — and translates it into ceramic form. A table set with Sa7ne pieces is a table that could only belong to you.
