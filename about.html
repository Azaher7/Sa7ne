Our Story
Your Table Tells Your Guests Your Story
Sa7ne is not just dinnerware. It is the vocabulary of your home — handcrafted, personalised, and entirely yours.
The Origin
A Gift I Didn't Expect

I am a full-time lawyer. My days are spent inside a corporate 9-to-6, where stress is folded into my everyday routine. Over the years, ceramics has become a scheduled weekend ritual. Every Saturday or Sunday, I attend my scheduled therapy session. The attendees? It is just clay and... me.

If you have ever worked with clay, you know how grounding the experience is. It feels like you are in kindergarten again, playing communally in the class sandbox — except... you are an adult, in your 30s now, and you are willingly scheduling time into your weekend to play with adult play-doh.

As my experience with clay grew, my pieces became more distinct — more unique, more me. I created the sa7ne_ Instagram page. My friends took notice of my scheduled therapy sessions and the pieces I posted to the sa7ne Instagram page, and started asking me to dress their dinner table with my pieces.

What had started as a personal outlet was slowly becoming something more. One night, after an afternoon spent at the ceramics studio, I stopped to pray Isha at a beautiful mosque nearby. The mosque is a striking building tucked, almost improbably, into the heart of industrial Al Quoz, its exterior engraved with Quranic verses.

For months, I never looked closely at the mosque exterior and assumed the calligraphy running along the walls was Allah's 99 names. This was not an unreasonable guess, given that the interior of the Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi is adorned the same way. As I walked into the mosque, I looked up and found الرحمان — Ar-Rahman beautifully written. Leaving the mosque, I looked up again and noticed these were full ayahs — verses of the Qur'an. It was not the 99 names of Allah — it was Surah Ar-Rahman, which happens to be my favourite surah in the Quran Al Karim. Reading through its verses line by line, I came across one I had somehow never paid attention to before, verse 14:

خَلَقَ الْإِنسَانَ مِنْ صَلْصَالٍ كَالْفَخَّارِ

"He created man from sounding clay, like pottery." — Surah Ar-Rahman, 55:14

How curious, that this verse should sit inside my favourite surah — and that I should find it on the very night I needed to. Allah places our inclinations, our temperaments, and His words exactly where they need to be. سبحان الله. Making that connection felt like a gift I hadn't asked for — هذا من فضل ربي, a favour of my Lord. That night I decided I must launch the Sa7ne website and share my humble and sincere passion for ceramics with all of you.

The Philosophy
Hosting Is Storytelling

There is a particular kind of host whose table you remember long after the evening ends. It is not the food alone — it is the feeling of intention. The sense that every object was chosen, that the colors speak to each other, that the table itself has a voice. Sa7ne exists to give that voice a form.

We think of each collection as a chapter in the homeowner's story. The blush speckled bowl that always holds the season's fruit. The honey-glazed platter that appears at every gathering. The cake stand painted with stars and the Arabic word for sustenance — رزق — set out on the night of a celebration. These pieces are not interchangeable. They belong to a specific home, a specific host, a specific way of being in the world.

Sa7ne captures the personality and vocabulary of the dinner host and hostess — their colour sensibility, their warmth, their humour, their heritage — and translates it into ceramic form. A table set with Sa7ne pieces is a table that could only belong to you.
